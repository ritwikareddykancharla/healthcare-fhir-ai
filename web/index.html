01 · Problem

Problem

Ambient clinical documentation and provider administrative workflows require strict transcript provenance, deterministic medication and negation verification, valid HL7 FHIR R4 encoding, and proactive payer evidence checks.

100% Synthetic Encounters

Overview

HealthScribeFHIR connects outpatient exam-room documentation to downstream interoperability and insurance clearance. Amazon Bedrock (us.amazon.nova-lite-v1:0) synthesizes structured Subjective / Objective / Assessment / Plan notes where every clinical claim cites exact utterance_id dialogue timestamps. Before a note reaches clinician sign-off, a deterministic Python verifier checks clinical polarity and medication dosing in 0.172 ms (p50), compiles verified findings into schema-checked HL7 FHIR R4 transaction Bundles in 0.016 ms, evaluates payer medical-necessity rules in 0.29 ms, and replaces all 18 HIPAA Safe-Harbor identifier classes with reversible HMAC-SHA256 vault tokens in 0.363 ms.

01 · FLIPPED CLINICAL POLARITY

Negated Symptoms Charted as Positive

When a patient states they have no chest pain and no shortness of breath, a single-token generative slip can chart positive dyspnea or angina, distorting risk scores and downstream clinical handoffs.

02 · MEDICATION DOSAGE DRIFT

Decimal, Unit, and Frequency Mismatches

Charting metformin 5000 mg instead of 500 mg, swapping mg with mcg, or recording weekly semaglutide as once daily creates preventable prescribing errors if left to free-text summarization.

03 · MALFORMED FHIR PAYLOADS

Broken References and Coding Systems

Downstream EHR ingestion rejects clinical payloads when Condition, Observation, or MedicationRequest entries omit required statuses, misuse ICD-10-CM, LOINC, or RxNorm URIs, or leave dangling urn:uuid: links.

04 · PREVENTABLE PAYER DENIALS

Missing Step-Therapy Evidence at Order Time

Ordering a lumbar spine MRI (CPT 72148) without documenting 6 weeks of conservative physical therapy or neurologic red flags triggers administrative claim denials days after the patient leaves the clinic.

03 · Implementation

Implementation

HealthScribeFHIR separates probabilistic clinical note drafting from deterministic polarity, dosage, HL7 FHIR R4, payer prior-authorization, and HIPAA de-identification rules across five typed Python 3.12 modules.

12 / 12 Unit Tests Passing
01 · SPAN-LINKED SOAP SCRIBE & SAFETY GATE healthscribe_fhir/soap_scribe.py
Drafts structured Subjective / Objective / Assessment / Plan notes from synthetic multi-speaker outpatient encounters while linking every sentence to exact dialogue turns and blocking flipped negations or altered drug dosages.
generate_span_linked_soap() invokes Amazon Bedrock (us.amazon.nova-lite-v1:0) via the Converse API (1,693.0 ms p50 across 12 calls, 931.6 mean tokens). verify_clinical_claims() runs pre/post-window NegEx polarity analysis (skipping interrogative clinician screening questions) and regex dosage/unit/frequency extraction in 0.172 ms (p50), catching 30 / 30 adversarial clinical claims with 0 false positives.
02 · HL7 FHIR R4 BUNDLE COMPILER & VALIDATOR healthscribe_fhir/fhir_validator.py
Converts verified encounter entities into interoperable HL7 FHIR R4 transaction Bundles and blocks malformed clinical codes, missing mandatory fields, or broken cross-resource references before EHR ingestion.
compile_fhir_r4_bundle() emits UUIDv5-linked Patient, Practitioner (us-npi), Encounter, Condition (ICD-10-CM), Observation (LOINC + UCUM), and MedicationRequest (RxNorm) entries. validate_fhir_r4_bundle() checks cardinality, coding system URIs, UCUM units, and urn:uuid: referential integrity in 0.016 ms (p50), catching 12 / 12 injected schema and reference defects.
03 · PAYER MEDICAL-NECESSITY PRIOR AUTH healthscribe_fhir/prior_auth.py
Checks outpatient imaging and specialty medication orders against payer coverage policies while the patient is still in the exam room, highlighting exact missing documentation before a claim can be denied.
evaluate_prior_auth() evaluates deterministic policy trees for Lumbar Spine MRI (CPT 72148), GLP-1 Escalation (J3490-GLP1), and Myocardial Perfusion Imaging (CPT 78452) against compiled FHIR resources and dialogue spans in 0.29 ms (p50), achieving 8 / 8 accuracy across eligible, step-therapy-deficient, and red-flag bypass cases.
04 · HIPAA SAFE-HARBOR VAULT & AUDIT LEDGER phi_scrubber.py · audit_store.py · server.py
De-identifies clinical notes across all 18 HIPAA Safe-Harbor identifier classes for downstream analytics while keeping clinical vitals and drug dosages intact and recording every action in a tamper-evident log.
HipaaSafeHarborScrubber replaces names, dates more granular than year, ages over 89, MRNs, NPIs, SSNs, phones, faxes, emails, addresses, URLs, IPs, and device/biometric IDs with deterministic HMAC-SHA256 vault tokens (42 / 42 PHI spans redacted in 0.363 ms p50). SQLiteClinicalAuditStore chains events in SQLite WAL (90 rows verified in 0.642 ms).
04 · Trade-Offs

Trade-Offs

Every architectural choice balances clinical expressiveness, sub-millisecond verification latency, interoperability strictness, and serverless operating cost.

Design Decisions
01 · DETERMINISTIC SAFETY GATE VS LLM SELF-CRITIQUE

Rule-Based Polarity and Dose Verification Over Second-Pass Prompting

Using a second LLM call to review a drafted SOAP note adds another 1.5 seconds of latency and can overlook subtle 500 mg vs 5000 mg decimal shifts. Running NegEx polarity windowing and structured dosage checks in Python completes in 0.172 ms (p50) with deterministic reproduciblity, at the trade-off of maintaining explicit clinical synonym and drug lexicons.

02 · UTTERANCE-LOCKED CLAIMS VS FREE-FORM NARRATIVE

Mandatory Transcript Citations Over Unconstrained Prose

Requiring every SOAP claim to carry non-empty utterance_ids constrains stylistic prose freedom, because claims without direct spoken provenance fail CLIN-SPAN-01. In clinical documentation, that constraint lets physicians verify any sentence against the exact spoken turn in one click.

03 · SERVERLESS FHIR VALIDATOR VS MANAGED HEALTHLAKE

Zero Idle Infrastructure Cost vs Full Terminology Server

Managed FHIR datastores such as AWS HealthLake bill continuously by the hour even when idle. Compiling and validating HL7 FHIR R4 transaction Bundles inside Python 3.12 AWS Lambda + SQLite WAL keeps idle cost at $0.00 and validates bundles in 0.016 ms, trading away full 100,000-concept SNOMED-CT subsumption queries.

04 · REVERSIBLE HMAC VAULT VS ONE-WAY MASKING

Consistent Longitudinal Tokens Over Anonymous Placeholders

Replacing every patient name with a generic [REDACTED] string destroys longitudinal linkage across visits. Keyed HMAC-SHA256 vault tokens such as [PATIENT_8F2A] preserve cross-encounter analytics linkage and support audited clinician re-identification while requiring strict key custody and SQLite audit logging.
